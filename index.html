Why Groundnuts from India

Quality, Grades & Export Readiness.

01

Bold & Java Varieties

Our core range includes Bold and Java Groundnuts (pods and kernels), sourced from Rajasthan and Gujarat, India.

02

Multiple Size Grades

Bold Groundnut is available in pod sizes 18/20, 20/24 and 20/26, with kernel sizes from 40/50 to 70/80. Java Groundnut is available in pod sizes 40/50, 45/55, 50/60 and 60/70, with kernel sizes from 40/50 to 80/90.

03

Application-Specific Selection

Groundnuts can be selected for applications including roasting, snacking, peanut butter, confectionery, bakery and other food uses.

04

Export Quality Parameters

Buyers can specify requirements such as grade, moisture, aflatoxin, kernel quality and other product parameters.

05

Flexible Packaging

Packaging can include PP or jute bags, with other options discussed according to buyer requirements.

06

International Market Focus

We focus on serving international buyers looking for Indian groundnuts and specifications suited to their requirements.
