PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Premium Indian Java Groundnuts

Java Groundnuts are a small, round Indian groundnut variety characterized by their pinkish skin and compact kernel size. They are available in multiple commercial grades and are used across a range of food applications.

Java Groundnuts are commonly used for peanut butter, confectionery, dry roasting, chikki and brittle, bakery products and blanching applications.

Key Highlights

  • ✔ Small & Round Kernels
  • ✔ Pinkish Skin
  • ✔ Multiple Commercial Grades
  • ✔ Peanut Butter Applications
  • ✔ Confectionery & Bakery Uses
  • ✔ Suitable for Dry Roasting
