OUR STORY

From Rajasthan & Gujarat to Global Buyers

Rajson Impex focuses on supplying Indian groundnuts to international buyers, with Bold and Java Groundnuts sourced from Rajasthan and Gujarat. Our product range includes multiple commercial grades to suit different buyer and food-processing requirements.

Our groundnut range is available in different grades and packaging options, allowing buyers to specify their preferred product, quantity and packing requirements according to their needs.

Our focus is on providing clear product information and supporting buyers with the details needed for an export enquiry, from variety and grade to quantity, packaging and destination.

Meet the Founder

Rajson Impex was founded under the leadership of Mr. Rajendra Jakhar, with a vision of connecting Indian agricultural products with buyers around the world through quality, trust and consistent service.

His 35+ years of experience, dedication to customer satisfaction and commitment to ethical business practices continue to guide Rajson Impex today, with a focus on building long-term relationships with buyers.
